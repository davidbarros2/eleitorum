<objective>
Build the three lower-half wizard step widgets that handle processing and result display: `StepProcessing` (step 3.5, the indeterminate→determinate progress screen with Cancel + D-01 confirmation), `StepPreview` (step 4, the preview table with summary panel and "Ver detalhes" log toggle), and `StepDone` (step 6, the dual-state success/error widget with "Abrir pasta" and "Processar outro ficheiro"). Each widget consumes PipelineWorker signals or PipelineResult data; none of them launch the pipeline themselves — that orchestration belongs to wizard.py in plan 06.

Purpose: Encapsulate the most behaviorally complex widgets (cross-thread signal handling, dual-state visual, large log scrolling) as independent, testable units before plan 06 wires them together via wizard.py.

Output: Three step widgets + three pytest-qt smoke tests, all green.
</objective>
